## Dataset: why safety-class F1 is 0.56

The generator prompt (`llm/prompts.py:47-66`) asks for "how serious it is" in everyday
words but never receives the immediate / urgent / routine definitions; the extractor prompt
(`llm/prompts.py:129-133`) does. Texts written for `urgent` describe FS17 immediate
hazards ("power points sparking", "sewer waste coming up in the yard", "back door swings
open after a break-in"), and the extractor reads them as immediate: 386 of 652 urgent
labels come back immediate. The headline weakness (Sonnet 0.61 accuracy vs TF-IDF 0.85 on
safety class) is mostly label noise from the generator, not extractor error.
Fix: give the generator the same class definitions and regenerate, or adjudicate the
disagreements against FS17 by hand and report both.
